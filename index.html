About these records

What this directory publishes, what it deliberately withholds, and how to verify a commission independently.

Source of record

Compiled from the Florida Department of State's published Notaries Public daily journals — a free public record released by the state in machine-readable form. Records are refreshed on an ongoing basis.

Information withheld

We publish name, commission series and number, issue and expiration date, bonding agency, city, county, and ZIP. We do not publish street addresses, telephone numbers, or dates of birth, and we exclude records carrying an address restriction under Fla. Stat. §119.071(4)(d).

Independent verification

A listing here is a convenience, not an official certification. Verify any commission directly through the Florida Department of State notary search before relying on it for a transaction.

Statutory fee limits

Florida caps notarial act fees at $10 per traditional act and $25 per online act (Fla. Stat. §§117.05, 117.275). Travel, printing, and signing-agent service fees are separate and negotiated directly with the notary.

Corrections and removal

Any notary may claim, correct, or request removal of a listing at no cost. Removal here does not affect the underlying state record, which remains public.

No legal advice

This directory does not provide legal advice, and neither may a Florida notary public. A notary may not select documents, explain their legal effect, or advise on how to complete them.
