Where this data comes from. Listings are built from the Florida Department of State's published Notaries Public daily journals, a free public record. We deliberately publish only name, notary ID, commission series and number, issue and expiration date, bonding agency, city, county, and ZIP. We do not publish street addresses, telephone numbers, or dates of birth, and we exclude any record flagged with an address restriction under Fla. Stat. §119.071(4)(d). Any notary may claim, correct, or request removal of their listing at any time.

State Notary Agent® is a directory and referral service. Listed notaries are independent contractors, not employees, and a listing is not an endorsement. Always verify a commission directly with the Florida Department of State before relying on it. We do not provide legal advice — and Florida notaries public may not either.

Florida caps notarial act fees at $10 per traditional act and $25 per online act (Fla. Stat. §§117.05, 117.275). Travel, printing, and signing-agent service fees are separate and negotiated directly with the notary.
