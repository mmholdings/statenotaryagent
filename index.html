State Notary Agent® is a directory and referral service. Listed notaries are independent contractors, not employees. State Notary Agent does not provide legal advice and does not select, prepare, or explain the legal effect of documents — Florida notaries public may not do so either.

Florida caps notarial act fees at $10 per traditional act and $25 per online act (Fla. Stat. §§117.05, 117.275). Travel, printing, and signing-agent service fees are separate and negotiated between you and the agent.
